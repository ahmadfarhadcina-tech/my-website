THE VISION

ABOUT CINER

01 / 04
A CINA GAMES PROJECT

Ciner is an ambitious open-world action game created by Cina Games. Set in a living world filled with danger, conflict, crime and unexpected events, Ciner focuses on freedom, exploration and a story-driven experience.

Players will step into a world where every decision can lead to new situations. From intense missions and dangerous encounters to exploring the streets and discovering hidden places, the world of Ciner is designed to feel alive and unpredictable.

Ciner is being developed as a long-term project with the goal of creating a memorable open-world experience inspired by the freedom and scale of modern action games.

THE STORY

03 / 04

Ciner tells a story of ambition, loyalty, danger and survival in a world where power can change everything.

As the story unfolds, Ciner will face dangerous situations, powerful enemies and difficult choices. His journey will take him through different parts of the city and deeper into a world where nothing is as simple as it first appears.

Along the way, important characters will become part of Ciner's journey, each with their own motivations, relationships and secrets.
